THE DETAILS

Privacy information.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

When you use our inquiry form, we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, business name, and project description.

What the information is used for

We use inquiry details to review and respond to your request, discuss potential services, and follow up about a project you contacted us about.

How messages are sent

When the inquiry form is enabled, submissions are transmitted securely to our form delivery service and emailed to our business inbox using Resend, a third-party email delivery provider. Information is processed as needed to deliver the message.

Analytics and cookies

This website does not include a custom analytics or advertising tracker by default. Our hosting provider may process standard technical request data needed to operate and secure the service. Live demo previews load third-party websites; those destinations may process their own request data.

Contact about your information

If you want to ask about information you provided, please use the contact page. This notice is a starting-point description and should be reviewed against the business’s final operating practices before launch.
